🎯 Today's Mission / Misi Hari Ini

"Your mission is to connect ideas and create your own English sentences!"

Gabungkan ide-ide sederhana menjadi kalimat Bahasa Inggris yang padu dan benar menggunakan kata hubung yang tepat.

AND ➕

Add an idea

Menambahkan informasi yang sejalan atau setara.

Idea A + Idea B
I like rice AND noodles.
BUT ⚡

Show a contrast

Menunjukkan pertentangan atau perbedaan.

Idea A ⇸ Idea B
I am tired, BUT I study.
SO ➡️

Show a result

Menunjukkan akibat atau hasil dari situasi.

Cause ➔ Result
I was hungry, SO I ate.

📌 Learning Objectives / Tujuan Pembelajaran

  • 1. Explain the function of AND, BUT, SO in simple terms. (Menjelaskan fungsi kata hubung).
  • 2. Distinguish between addition, contrast, and result relationships. (Membedakan jenis hubungan kalimat).
  • 3. Select the correct linking word based on context. (Memilih kata hubung yang tepat).
  • 4. Combine two sentences into one well-structured sentence. (Menggabungkan dua kalimat).
  • 5. Create original sentences about daily life & school. (Membuat kalimat karya sendiri).
  • 6. Earn an individual assessment report score. (Mendapatkan laporan nilai akhir).

Pedagogical Note for Class VIII G Remedial:

Students struggle primarily with confusing BUT (contrast) vs SO (result). Emphasize that BUT alters expectation while SO delivers the logical outcome.

🔑 Quick Answer Key Reference

Grammar Detective:

  • Q1: Addition (and)
  • Q2: Result (so)
  • Q3: Contrast (but)

Final Challenge Key:

  1. I like apples [and] bananas. (Addition)
  2. It was hot outside, [so] we went swimming. (Result)
  3. He ran fast, [but] he missed the bus. (Contrast)
  4. She studied hard, [so] she passed the exam. (Result)
  5. Combine (Cats/Rabbits): AND
  6. Combine (Rain/Inside): SO
  7. Combine (Young/Responsible): BUT
  8. Forgot homework: SO
